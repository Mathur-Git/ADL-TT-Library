# On Balance Volume (OBV)

> Category: **Analytics** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/analytics/charts/technical-indicators/on-balance-volume-obv/)

On Balance Volume (OBV) maintains a cumulative running total of the amount of volume occurring on up periods compared to down periods.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/on-balance-volume.png)

## Configuration Options

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/on-balance-volume-1.png)

* **Color Selectors**: Colors to use for graph elements.
* **Display Axis Label**: Whether to display the most recent value on the Y axis.

## Formula

\[ OBV = Cumulative(Up\;Volume – Down\;Volume) \]

Where:

* Up Volume = Quantity of volume occurring on up price change
* Down Volume = Quantity of volume occurring on down price change
