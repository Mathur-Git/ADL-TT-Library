# Volume Rate of Change

> Category: **Analytics** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/analytics/charts/technical-indicators/volume-rate-of-change/)

The Volume Rate of Change indicator measures the rate of change in the volume over a period of time.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/volume-rate-of-change.png)

## Configuration Options

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/volume-rate-of-change-1.png)

* **Period**: Number of bars to use in the calculations.
* **Color Selectors**: Colors to use for graph elements.
* **Display Axis Label**: Whether to display the most recent value on the Y axis.

## Formula

[ VROC = frac{Volume – Volume\_text{previous-period}}{Volume\_text{previous-period}} times 100 ]
