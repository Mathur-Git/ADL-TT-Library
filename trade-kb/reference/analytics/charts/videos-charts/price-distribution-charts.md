# Price Distribution Charts

> Category: **Analytics** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/analytics/charts/videos-charts/price-distribution-charts/)

## Video Summary

TT’s Price Distribution charts illustrate the distribution of price over time for a given instrument. Using visual indicators placed in line with the price levels at which an instrument has traded, traders can easily view the relationship between price action and trade volume across multiple days, over the course of each individual day as well as during a specific portion of the day.
