# Trading Crypto on TT Overview

> Category: **Basic Order Entry** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/basic-order-entry/trading-crypto-on-tt/description-trading-crypto-on-tt/trading-crypto-on-tt-overview/)

The TT® platform provides users with access to crypto exchanges to trade a variety of digital currencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Litecoin.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/gdx-overview-cb.png)

## Getting Started

Before you begin trading on TT, you’ll need to create an account and complete the onboarding process at
the crypto exchange where you wish to trade.

After completing those tasks, you’ll need to set up your access to TT. The onboarding process varies based on user needs. If you are member of a trading firm, an individual user managed by a firm, or have your own
“company” in TT and want to trade futures in addition to cryptocurrencies, refer to [Trading Firm Users](trading-firm-users.md).
