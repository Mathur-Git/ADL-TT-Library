# Order and Fills widget

> Category: **Order Management** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/order-management/orders-and-fills/reference-orders-and-fills/order-and-fills-widget/)

The Order and Fills widget combines functionality from the Order Book, Fills and Positions widgets so you can monitor and manage working orders, fills and positions in a single widget. This video gives an overview of the Order and Fills widget and shows how to use it to confirm orders and export a summary of filled orders to share with customers.
