# Account List Widget

> Category: **Order Management** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/order-management/account-list/videos-account-list/account-list-widget/)

## Video Summary

The Account List widget makes it easy for traders to monitor and execute trades for a large number of accounts. In this video, we’ll demonstrate how to use the Account List widget to select the account for order entry widgets and filters within widgets, customize the display of our account names and configure the widgets in the workspace to respond to account selection in the Accounts List widget.
