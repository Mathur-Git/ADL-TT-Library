# Viewing historical data

> Category: **Order Management** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/order-management/audit-trail/task-audit-trail/viewing-historical-data/)

You can view messages from any date and time in the history of the
account. Simply right-click in the widget and select **Go to date** from the context menu.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/AuditTrailWidgetCalendar.png)

When you scroll to the bottom of the Audit Trail, the **Load older records** button may be displayed. You can click this button to retrieve the next batch of data in chronological order.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/audit-trail-load-button.png)
