# Viewing alerts

> Category: **Order Management** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/order-management/alert-manager-and-alert-viewer/task-alert-manager-and-alert-viewer/viewing-alerts/)
>
> **Interpreted in:** [Order Management & Risk § Alerts (real-time risk guard)](../../../../guides/order-management-and-risk.md#alerts-real-time-risk-guard)

When enabled, pop-up alert notifications appear in the top right corner of your TT workspace when the alert is triggered. Each notification shows the color and message text defined for the alert in the Alert Manager, and shows how many times the notification has been sent.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/alrt-notifications.png)

When a notification appears, you can pin it to your workspace and click the drop-down arrow on the alert notification to show the **Pause** and **Edit** buttons. When you click **Edit**, the **Alert Detail** screen opens and you can [modify the alert](creating-an-alert.md).

When you click **Pause** in the alert notification, the alert is paused in the Alert Manager widget. The **Status** column displays “Paused”, and the alert row is grayed out. To resume the alert and resume sending notifications for the alert, click the ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/alrt-play-button-1.png) (Run) button in the Alert Manager.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/alrt-pause.png)

## Viewing historical alerts

To view all alerts that have triggered, right-click in the Alert Viewer and click **Show historical alerts**.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/alrt-historical.png)

Alerts that appear after the Alert Viewer is opened or historical fills are shown can be cleared using
the **Clear** button (historical alerts are not cleared).

To show historical alerts at a certain time and date, right-click in the Alert Viewer while viewing all alerts and click **Go to date…** in the context menu. This opens the **Go to:** date picker, which allows you to enter or select a time and date.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/alrt-goto-date.png)

## Exporting alert rows

To export alerts to a CSV file, press Shift on the keyboard and select one of more alerts, then right-click and select **export rows**.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/alrt-export.png)
