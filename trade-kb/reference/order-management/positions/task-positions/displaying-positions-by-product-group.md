# Displaying positions by product group

> Category: **Order Management** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/order-management/positions/task-positions/displaying-positions-by-product-group/)

You can use the Positions widget in [matrix view mode](../description-positions/positions-matrix-view.md) to organize similar products into user-defined groups and aggregate their positions. Product rows can be combined with other product rows or added to an existing group row.

To display positions by product group:

1. Right click in the widget and select **View Mode** | **Matrix**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pos-matrix-select-mode.png)
2. Left click one or more rows in the grid.

   **Tip**: To select multiple rows, left click and drag down across multiple rows or use Shift + left click or CTRL + left click.
3. Right-click the selected rows and click **Grouping** | **Create Group**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pos-matrix-create-group.png)
4. Enter a unique group name and click **Save**.

   Names can be up to 50 alphanumeric characters.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pos-matrix-group-name.png)

   The saved grouping appears in the left column. Selected groups can be renamed using the **Grouping** | **Rename Group** option.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pos-matrix-group-display-1.png)

## Adding and removing products in Positions matrix view

In “Matrix” view mode, you can add or remove one or more products to an existing product grouping. To add, select one or more rows in the grid, right click, and click **Grouping** | **Add to group** to select the group name.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pos-matrix-group-add.png)

To remove a product, select the product in the group, right-click and select **Grouping** | **Remove from group**.
