> Category: **Algo Trading** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/algo-trading/excel-integration-with-tt/videos-excel-integration-with-tt/excel-integration/)

## Video Summary

TT’s Microsoft Excel Add-In allows you to leverage the power of Excel by pulling live market data and position information from TT’s RTD formula engine. In this video we will Install and configure the Microsoft Excel Add-in and connect a spreadsheet to our workspace.

**Note:**

Though this video lists Excel 2013 as the requirement, support has been added for Microsoft Excel 2010.
