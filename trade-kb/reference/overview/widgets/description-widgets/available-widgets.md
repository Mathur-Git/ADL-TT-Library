# Available Widgets

> Category: **Overview** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/overview/widgets/description-widgets/available-widgets/)

The following widgets are available in the Trade application:

* [Account List](../../../order-management/account-list/description-account-list/account-list-overview.md) — Provides you the ability to quickly seed order entry screens and filter order and fill management widgets with specific accounts.
* [Aggregator](../../../spread-trading/aggregator/description-aggregator/aggregator-overview.md) — Provides you with the ability to create a single aggregated instrument made up of multiple contracts and trade it as a single instrument.
* [Alert Manager](../../../order-management/alert-manager-and-alert-viewer/description-alert-manager-and-alert-viewer/alert-manager-and-alert-viewer-overview.md) — Supports creating custom alerts and sending alert notifications to your workspace and TT mobile device.
* [Allocation](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/all-allocation-overview.html) — Used for assigning unreleased fills to child care orders of combined bulk orders using a selected allocation method.
* [Algo Dashboard](../../../algo-trading/algo-dashboard/description-algo-dashboard/algo-dashboard-overview.md) — Gives you the ability to launch and manage all of your algos from a single display.
* [Assets](../../../basic-order-entry/trading-crypto-on-tt/description-trading-crypto-on-tt/assets-widget.md) — View crypto exchange wallet balances using the Assets widget.
* [Audit Query](../../../order-management/audit-query/description-audit-query/audit-query-overview.md) — Searches the audit trail data in TT and filters the results based on your custom queries.
* [Audit Trail](../../../order-management/audit-trail/description-audit-trail/audit-trail-overview.md) — Displays all of your historical account activity.
* [Autospreader](../../../spread-trading/autospreader/description-autospreader/introduction-to-autospreader.md) — Gives you the ability to create synthetic spreads and launch them on a server for low-latency execution.
* [Autospreader Rules](../../../spread-trading/autospreader/description-autospreader/autospreader-rules-overview.md) — Gives you the ability to customize the quoting and hedging logic for your Autospreader spreads.
* [Autotrader](../../../algo-trading/autotrader/description-autotrader/autotrader-overview.md) — Helps you quickly set up and launch one or more instances of an algo.
* [Balances](../../../order-management/balances/description-balances/balances-overview.md) — Allows you to view real-time account balances and margin requirements of your account.
* [Blocktrader](../../../basic-order-entry/blocktrader/description-blocktrader/blocktrader-overview.md) — Gives you the ability to submit wholesale and OTC trades.
* [Chart](../../../analytics/charts/description-charts/chart-overview.md) — Displays historical market data in a variety of chart types and gives you the ability to add a multitude of technical indicators.
* [Depth](/trade/dep-depth-widget-overview.html%0A) — Supports viewing detailed or aggregate depth for a single instrument.
* [Electronic Eye](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/oee-options-electronic-eye-overview.html) — Displays market data for all puts and calls of an options expiry and can be configured to find trading opportunities based on your criteria.
* [Exchange News](/trade/b3-trading-on-b3-overview.html%0A) — Displays news messages sent by the B3 exchange via their price feed.
* [Expiration Manager](../../../order-management/position-manager/description-position-manager/position-manager-overview.md) — Allows you to monitor your positions by strike and easily and accurately expire your options positions.
* [Fills](../../../order-management/fills/description-fills/fills-overview.md) — Displays all of your fills from the current trading session as well as from previous trading sessions.
* [Hedge Manager](../../../spread-trading/hedge-manager/description-hedge-manager/hedge-manager-overview.md) — Displays all of your Autospreader hedge orders in a single widget and gives you the ability to act on unfilled hedge orders.
* [Market Grid](../../../viewing-market-data/market-grid/description-market-grid/introduction-to-market-grid.md) — Displays market data for multiple instruments in a grid-like format and gives you the ability to trade those instruments.
* [MD Trader](../../../basic-order-entry/md-trader/description-md-trader/md-trader-overview.md) — The industry-standard ladder displays market depth for a single instrument and gives you the ability to quickly and safely trade with a single click.
* [Options Chain](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/opc-overview.html) — Displays a listing of the call and put option strike prices along with their premiums for a given maturity period.
* [Options Risk](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/or-options-risk-overview.html) — Displays your open position in the options contracts and underlying futures contracts for a product on a per account basis.
* [Options Trade Monitor](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/otm-options-trade-monitor-overview.html) — Displays all real-time and historical trades that occur for a selected family of products and provides details for each trade.
* [Orders and Fills](../../../order-management/orders-and-fills/description-orders-and-fills/orders-and-fills-overview.md) — Helps you easily monitor and manage your working orders and fills.
* [Order Book](../../../order-management/order-book/description-order-book/order-book-overview.md) — Displays all of your working orders and gives you the ability to cancel or modify those orders.
* [Order Exceptions](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/oe-order-exceptions-overview.html) — Enables you to efficiently manage and resolve FIX care order rejections.
* [Positions](../../../order-management/positions/description-positions/positions-overview.md) — Displays your positions by account, exchange, or product.
* [Position Manager](../../../order-management/position-manager/description-position-manager/position-manager-overview.md) — Allows you to create and publish manual fills and Start of Day (SOD) records for users and accounts in your company.
* [Risk Matrix](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/orm-options-risk-matrix-overview.html) — Shows you a high-level view of your options position across multiple scenarios at once.
* [RFQ Viewer](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/rfq-viewer-overview.html) — provides you with the ability to monitor and act upon an RFQ (request for quote) for an instrument.
* [Spread Matrix](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/sm-overview.html) — Provides you the ability to view market data for multiple instruments and the spreads between those outright instruments.
* [Strategy Creation](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/sc-strategy-creation-overview.html) — Provides you the ability to create custom, user-defined strategies and submit them to the exchange.
* [Template Manager](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/tm-overview.html) — Lets you quickly create, modify and manage algo templates for your algos.
* [Time & Sales](../../../viewing-market-data/time-and-sales/description-time-and-sales/time-sales-overview.md) — Displays trade activity for one or more instruments.
* [Trader Analytics](../../../analytics/trader-analytics/description-trader-analytics/trader-analytics-overview.md) — Gives a risk manager or trader the ability to analyze the current day’s trades and historical trade data from a comprehensive statistics report.
* [Vol Curve Manager](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/vcm-overview.html) — Displays multiple volatility curves for an underlying instrument and gives users the ability to create their own volatility curve.
* [Watchlist](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/wl-watchlist-overview.html) — Provides you with the ability to manage and monitor market data for selected options and futures instruments, as well as monitor Greeks and theoretical values of user-defined options strategies before actually submitting them to the market.
