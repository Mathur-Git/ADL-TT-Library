# Reporting an incident or sending feedback

> Category: **Overview** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/overview/widgets/task-widgets/reporting-an-incident-or-sending-feedback/)

To report an incident or send feedback:

1. Right-click in the widget’s title bar and select **Contact support…** in the context menu.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-context.png)

   **Tip**: Click **? Support** on the workspace menu bar and click **Send Feedback** on the TT Support menu to open a Feedback widget with a screenshot of your entire workspace.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-context1.png)
2. In the left pane of the open Feedback widget, describe the issue or provide feedback for the corresponding widget.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-widget.png)

   If you do not want to include a screenshot and data regarding your environment,
   uncheck **Include screenshot and data**.

   This option is checked (enabled) by default and a screenshot of the widget with corresponding meta data is sent to TT with your feedback.

   **Note**: Including the screenshot and data helps TT more quickly diagnose your issue.
3. If you have a widget screenshot but want to send a screenshot of your entire workspace, click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-camera-1.png) in the right pane.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-camera.png)

   **Tip**: After capturing your workspace, you can click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-camera-2.png) to toggle between the widget and workspace screenshots.

   You can also choose to [hide](#hide) and [highlight](#highlight) parts of the screenshots that you submit with your feedback.
4. Optionally, click the **Data** tab and review the meta data that will be sent to TT.
5. Click **Submit**.

### Hiding parts of a screenshot

To hide parts of a screenshot:

1. Click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-edit-1.png) (the edit icon) in the lower right-pane and click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-hide-1.png).
2. Move the cursor to a region on the screenshot and click-and-drag the cursor.

   A black box is displayed to cover up the selected region on the screenshot.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-hide.png)

   To undo any changes, click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-undo-1.png).
3. To save the changes, click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-save-3.png).

### Highlighting parts of a screenshot

To highlight parts of a screenshot:

1. Click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-edit-2.png) (the edit icon) in the lower right-pane and click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-highlight-1.png).
2. Move the cursor to a region on the screenshot and click-and-drag the cursor.

   The selected region is highlighted and the rest of the screenshot is shaded. Click-and-drag the cursor to adjust the size of the highlighted region.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-highlight.png)

   To undo any changes, click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-undo-2.png).
3. To save the changes, click ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/icon-save-4.png).

## Sending feedback for Fills records

To send feedback for Fills records:

1. Highlight one or more records in an opened Fills widget.
2. Right-click in the highlighted records and select **Contact support…** in the context menu.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-fills1.png)

   The **Feedback** widget opens for the selected records. The description in the left pane is pre-populated with a feedback request.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trade-feedback-fills2.png)
3. Optionally, [highlight](#highlight) parts of the screenshot or [hide](#hide) any personal data on the screenshot.
4. Click **Submit**.
