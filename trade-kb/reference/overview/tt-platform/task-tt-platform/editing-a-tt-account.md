# Editing a TT account

> Category: **Overview** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/overview/tt-platform/task-tt-platform/editing-a-tt-account/)

You can easily edit your existing account to:

* Update your name
* Change your email address
* Update the company name

To edit a TT account:

1. Access your account settings. If you are already logged into TT, click your username in the top right side of the title bar. Otherwise, navigate to <http://id.tradingtechnologies.com> and enter your TT account credentials.
2. Click the **EDIT** button in the Account Settings window.

   ![](/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tt-edit-acc1.png)
3. Update the desired information.

   ![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/tt-edit-acc2.png)
4. Click the **Save** button.

   A message displays: “Your changes have been saved.”
