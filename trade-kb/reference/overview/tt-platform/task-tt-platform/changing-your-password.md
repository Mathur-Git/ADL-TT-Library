# Changing your password

> Category: **Overview** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/overview/tt-platform/task-tt-platform/changing-your-password/)
>
> **Interpreted in:** [Platform & Workspace § TT accounts](../../../../guides/platform-and-workspace.md#tt-accounts)

To change your password:

1. Click your account username in the title bar to open the **Account Settings** page.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/acct-settings-1.png)
2. On the **Account Settings** page, click the **Security** tab and click **CHANGE PASSWORD**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/password-change-step1.png)
3. Enter your current and new passwords and click **SAVE**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/password-change-step2.png)
