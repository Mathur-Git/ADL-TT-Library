# Creating a TT account

> Category: **Overview** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/overview/tt-platform/task-tt-platform/creating-a-tt-account/)

The first step to accessing the TT Platform trading environment is to create a TT account (TT ID).

To create a TT account:

1. Go to [http://www.trade.tt](https://www.trade.tt) to begin
2. In the **NEW USER?** section of **THE TT PLATFORM** page, enter your email address and
   click **CREATE ACCOUNT**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tt-new-user.png)
3. Complete the account creation form and click **CREATE ACCOUNT**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tt-form2.png)

   Passwords must be a minimum of twelve characters and include three of the following:

   * A lowercase letter
   * An uppercase letter
   * A number
   * A special character

   After creating an account, you’ll receive an email requesting you to “Confirm Email”. Check your junk and spam folders for the email if you do not receive it immediately.
4. In the registration email you received, click **Confirm Email**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tt-confirm-email.png)
5. Read and accept the service agreement.
6. Log in to trade.tt using your new account name (TTID) and password.
