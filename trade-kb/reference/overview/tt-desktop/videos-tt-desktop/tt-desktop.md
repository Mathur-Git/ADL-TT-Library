## Video Summary

TT Desktop gives you the ability to access the TT platform on your Windows computer with an application that is designed for a multi-monitor setup and provides the highest level of performance. In this video we will show you how to download and install TT Desktop and demonstrate you how to create a multi-monitor workspace.
