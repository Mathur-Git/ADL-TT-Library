# Logging in to TT

> Category: **Overview** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/overview/browser-access/task-browser-access/logging-in-to-tt/)

To log in to TT:

1. Go to <https://trade.tt>.
2. Enter your TT Account credentials.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tt-login.png)

   **Note**: If you have not set up [two-step verification](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/ttp-configuring-two-step-verification.html) in your account settings and two-step verification is enabled for your company by your administrator, you will be prompted to configure your settings when attempting to log in to TT® from any
   [supported browser](../../tt-platform/description-tt-platform/tt-accounts.md).

   **Note:** The TT front end application enforces the idle timeout inactivity setting as optionally defined in the Setup application.

   **Note:** A status message will be displayed if you attempt to log in and exceed the number of allowed concurrent login sessions.
