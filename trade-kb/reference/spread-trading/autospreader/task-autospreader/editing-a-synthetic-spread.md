# Editing a Synthetic Spread

> Category: **Spread Trading** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/spread-trading/autospreader/task-autospreader/editing-a-synthetic-spread/)

To edit a synthetic spread:

1. Click the edit icon next to a spread in the Autospreader widget.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/as-edit.png)

   The **Edit Spread: (spread name)**
   screen appears.
2. Make the changes to the spread as needed and click **Save**.

   If prompted to confirm unsaved changes, click **Save Changes**.
