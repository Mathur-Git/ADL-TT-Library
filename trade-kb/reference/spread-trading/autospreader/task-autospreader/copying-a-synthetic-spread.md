# Copying a Synthetic Spread

> Category: **Spread Trading** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/spread-trading/autospreader/task-autospreader/copying-a-synthetic-spread/)

To copy an Autospreader spread:

1. Click the checkbox next to the spread you wish to copy.
2. Click the **Copy** button.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/as-copy.png)
3. Click the pencil icon to edit the new spread
4. Provide a new name, color and adujst parameters
5. Click **Save**

Note When copying a shared spread, Autospreader Rules are also copied.
