# Hedge Manager Overview

> Category: **Spread Trading** · [KB Home](../../../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/spread-trading/hedge-manager/videos-hedge-manager/hedge-manager-overview-2/)

Monitor and manage your unfilled Autospreader hedge orders with Hedge Manager. With Hedge Manager you can view your working hedge orders, make adjustments to these orders, payup a tick, cross the market, apply an Order Management Algo and more.

Note: The Control Panel shown in the video is no longer available in the Hedge Manager.
