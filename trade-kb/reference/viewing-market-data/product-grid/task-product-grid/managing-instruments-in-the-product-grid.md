# Managing instruments in the Product Grid

> Category: **Viewing Market Data** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/viewing-market-data/product-grid/task-product-grid/managing-instruments-in-the-product-grid/)

## Adding products to the Product Grid

To add products to the Product Grid:

1. In the **Search** box, type the name of the product you wish to add or use the **Explore**
   button to drill down to the desired product by selecting the **Exchange**, **Family**, **Type**, then **Product**.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pg-add-product-search.png)

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pg-add-product-explore.png)
2. The selected products are added to the Product Grid.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pg-add-product-added.png)
3. Use **Search** or the **Explore** button to add additional products. Newly
   added products appear on the right.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pg-add-product-second.png)

## Removing a product from the Product Grid

Note There must be at least two products in the Product Grid to access the
option to remove a product.

To remove an existing product from Product Grid:

1. Right-click the header row of the product you want to remove and select **Remove Product
   Selection** from the context
   menu.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/pg-remove-product.png)
2. The selected product now longer appears in the Product Grid.
