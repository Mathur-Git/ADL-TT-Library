# Market Grid Display

> Category: **Viewing Market Data** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/viewing-market-data/market-grid/description-market-grid/market-grid-display/)

There are settings and options available in the Market Grid widget that provide the ability to visualize instruments and market data activity.

![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/mg-display1.png)

The following display options are available:

1. [Columns](../task-market-grid/configuring-the-market-grid-display.md) — Show or hide columns that are specific to your markets or instruments.
2. [Label Rows](../task-market-grid/configuring-the-market-grid-display.md) — Provide visual cues in a grid with many rows.
3. [Color rows by year](../task-market-grid/configuring-the-market-grid-display.md) — Differentiate instruments expiring in successive 12-month cycles.
4. [Highlight Updates](../task-market-grid/configuring-the-market-grid-display.md) — Highlight the **Last**, **Bid**, and **Ask** values when price updates occur.
5. [Tabs](../task-market-grid/using-tabs-in-the-market-grid.md) — Organize your instruments by showing and
   using tabs.
