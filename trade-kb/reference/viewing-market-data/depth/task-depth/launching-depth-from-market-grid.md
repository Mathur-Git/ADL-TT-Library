# Launching Depth from Market Grid

> Category: **Viewing Market Data** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/viewing-market-data/depth/task-depth/launching-depth-from-market-grid/)
>
> **Interpreted in:** [Market Data & Depth § Depth widget & book imbalance](../../../../guides/market-data-and-depth.md#depth-widget-book-imbalance)

The Depth widget can be launched as either a [stand-alone widget](#stand) or [linked widget](#link) per instrument from Market Grid.

## Opening a stand-alone Depth widget from Market Grid

To open a stand-alone Depth widget from Market Grid:

1. Add an instrument to Market Grid.
2. Right-click the instrument row and select **Open** | **Depth** from the context menu.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/depth-launch.png)

   The Depth widget opens seeded with market data and displays detailed depth for the instrument.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/depth-open-mg1.png)

## Launching a linked Depth widget from Market Grid

To launch a linked Depth widget from Market Grid:

1. Open an instrument in Market Grid.
2. Right-click the instrument row and select **Launch linked…** | **Depth** from the
   context menu.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/depth-linked1.png)

   A linked Depth widget opens seeded with market data and displays detailed depth for the instrument.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/depth-linked-mg1.png)
