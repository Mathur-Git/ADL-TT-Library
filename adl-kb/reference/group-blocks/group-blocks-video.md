# Group blocks video

> Category: **Group Blocks** · [KB Home](../../README.md) · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/adl/group-blocks/group-blocks-video/)

### Video Summary

Organize algos by placing multiple blocks into group blocks.

Group blocks represent a collection of blocks as a single block. In addition to decluttering our workspace, group blocks also help organize the algo blocks by purpose and let us more easily see how the different parts of our algo work. In this video, we will learn how to create a group block as well as how to define the inputs and outputs of our group block.
