# Opening ADL

> Category: **ADL Overview, Concepts & Tutorials** · [Source](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/adl/adl-overview/introduction-to-adl/task-introduction-to-adl/opening-adl/)

### Opening ADL®

To open ADL:

1. [Log in to TT](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/adl/gs-logging-in-to-the-trade-application.html) with your TT login.

   ![](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/gs-adl-in-titlebar.png)
2. In the title bar, click **ADL**, or open ADL from the TT Customer Portal [**Home** page](../../../../../trade-kb/reference/overview/browser-access/description-browser-access/home-page.md).

   A new browser window opens for ADL.

   ![Start ADL](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/gs-start-adl.png)
